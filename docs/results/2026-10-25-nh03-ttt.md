## Primary contrast (pre-registered decision)

**(b) − (a) = +4.70 points, 95 % bootstrap CI [+3.29, +6.08]**,
N = 30 paired seeds, SD 4.01, paired Hedges `g_z` = 1.14 ; 22/30 seeds
with a difference > 3 points, 1/30 below −3.

Decision rule §6 : mean > 3 and CI lower bound > 0 →
**"continuous > bounded"**. (b) reaches this with fewer estimated
FLOPs than (a) (3.8e7 vs 9.9e7), so the result also holds a fortiori
at equal FLOPs.

## Exploratory decomposition (clock vs substrate) — read this before citing the primary

| contrast | factor isolated | mean | 95 % CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A_continuous` − (a) | clock, slow substrate | **+15.70** | [+14.64, +16.83] |
| (b) − `B_ttt_chunked` | clock, TTT substrate | +6.94 | [+6.11, +7.69] |
| `B_ttt_chunked` − (a) | substrate, bounded clock | −2.24 | [−3.36, −1.14] |
| (b) − `A_continuous` (not pre-registered) | substrate, continuous clock | −11.00 | [−12.29, −9.76] |

The primary win of (b) over (a) is carried by the **clock**, not by the
TTT substrate : spreading the same 24 writes over wake time helps on
both substrates (+15.7 on slow weights, +6.9 on fast weights), while at
a fixed clock the fast-weight target is *worse* than writing the slow
weights (−2.2 bounded, −11.0 continuous). The best arm overall is
plain replay + downscale on the slow weights, spread continuously
(86.4).

## Reading

1. **The NH-03 statement "discrete ≡ continuous at equal budget" is
   refuted on this benchmark** : at an equal write budget the clock
   moves retention by up to 15.7 points. A dream episode is therefore
   *not* characterised by its budget alone ; where the writes fall in
   time matters.
2. Under the pre-registered rule the catalogue consequence applies
   literally ("continuous > bounded" → DR-0 as a write budget, TTT as
   5th substrate). The decomposition narrows it : the evidence
   supports rewriting DR-0 as a **write budget whose schedule is a
   free parameter** (bounded slots are one schedule among others, and
   not the best one here) ; it does **not** show that the TTT
   fast-weight module consolidates better than the existing substrate.
   `ttt_fast_weights` qualifies as a conforming substrate on the C+
   test suite (`tests/conformance/axioms/test_dr3_ttt_substrate.py`),
   independently of this ranking.
3. Likely mechanism, not tested here : interleaving writes with wake
   learning lets later writes repair interference created during the
   task, and the last continuous writes of task 4 fall closer to the
   final evaluation (a recency component). Disentangling interleaving
   from recency needs a schedule with all writes early-but-interleaved
   vs late-but-bounded.

## Limits

One toy benchmark (G4-ter MLP 784-32-16-2, Split-FMNIST), one TTT
variant (32 × 32 residual fast weights, supervised inner objective,
η = 0.01 calibrated on pilot seeds 1000–1004 to give (b) its best
shot), one consolidation HP set (G4-ter C5), N = 30, single host.
Synthetic-scale result : no claim about LLM-scale or biological sleep.

## Suggested follow-ups

- Schedule ablation (early-interleaved vs late-bounded) to separate
  interleaving from recency.
- Repeat the 2 × 2 (clock × substrate) on a second benchmark of the G4
  ladder (G4-quinto CIFAR CNN) before any DR-0 amendment.
- DR-0 amendment issue : wording "budget + schedule", with this note as
  evidence ; DualVer formal-axis bump only with the spec change.
